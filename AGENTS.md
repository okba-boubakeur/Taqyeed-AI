# Ulum Notes - Agent Guidelines & Repository Rules

## UI & Design Rules

### Monochrome Navigation & Toolbar Icons
- All action icons in navigation bars, bottom bars, toolbars, and selection menus must be **monochrome** (using `text-foreground hover:bg-muted` or matching the monochrome theme toggle icon).
- Do not use colored accent borders or colored icon fills (such as blue for share, amber for PDF, green for pin, or red for delete) on navigation and bottom bar buttons. Keep them clean, unified, and monochrome across both light and dark themes.
